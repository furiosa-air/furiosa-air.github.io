We work at the intersection of AI and hardware.

Our goal is to make AI computing sustainable. This site hosts project pages, code, and datasets for our research on efficient training and inference of large language models.

Research areas

What we work on

01

RL Post-Training

Reinforcement learning, distillation, and data curation that turn base models into capable reasoning and coding agents, with rollouts fast enough to scale.

02

Non-autoregressive Generation

Diffusion language models and parallel decoding that generate many tokens per step, along with the training and attention methods that make them practical.

03

Inference-time Algorithms

Speculative decoding, KV-cache compression, and test-time search that lower the latency and memory cost of serving large models.
